Why visit Lesotho?

Lesotho, the kingdom in the sky, is the only country in the world that lies entirely above 1000m. surrounded by South Africa, it offers unique culture, pony trekking, and snow in Africa.

  • Highest pub in Africa - Sani Mountain Lodge
  • Katse Dam - largest dam in Africa
  • Rich Basotho culture and blankets
