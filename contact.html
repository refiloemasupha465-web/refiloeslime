Frequently asked questions

Skiing at Afriski runs in the winter months, usually June to August. Hiking and pony trekking are best from September to May, when the weather is milder.

Visa rules depend on your nationality. Check with the Lesotho immigration office or your nearest embassy before you travel.

You can cancel a recent booking from the Booking page. For any other change, send us a message above and choose "Change or cancel a booking".

Pack warm layers, a waterproof jacket, sturdy shoes, sunscreen and a hat. Mountain weather changes quickly, even in summer.
